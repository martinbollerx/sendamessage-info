Privacy Policy

SendAMessage, a personal project by Martin · Last updated November 2026

Who this applies to

SendAMessage is a personal tool used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account. It is not offered to the public, and no other person's data is processed.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses the Google People API with the read-only scope contacts.readonly. It reads the names and birthdays of the contacts in the owner's own Google account. It does not read other contact fields, and it cannot create, change or delete contacts.

How the data is used

Once a day the app compares the birthdays with today's date and sends the owner one message through Telegram, naming the people whose birthday it is. The data is used for this purpose only.

Storage and sharing

  • Contact data is not stored. It is read, used to build the message, and discarded when the daily job ends. Names are not written to logs.
  • It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • The only place it goes is the owner's own Telegram chat, delivered through Telegram's bot service, which processes the message under its own privacy policy.
  • The app keeps an access credential (a refresh token) so it can read the contacts without a new login. It is stored in encrypted secret storage on the code-hosting service and on the owner's own computer.

Google API Services User Data Policy

SendAMessage's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions by choosing SendAMessage and selecting "Delete all connections". Without the credential, the app cannot read any contacts.
