SendAMessage

A personal project by Martin Not public

How it works

Step 1

Every morning at 09:00

Cloudflare (a timer in the cloud) triggers the job on GitHub Actions at 09:00 Berlin time.

Step 2

It looks at the contacts

It reads only names and birthdays from Martin's own Google Contacts via the Google People API. It can look, but it cannot change or delete anything.

Step 3

It finds today's birthdays

It compares each birthday with today's date. Nothing is saved, and the list is gone when the job ends.

Step 4

It sends a message

A Telegram message to Martin's phone, like this:

Good morning Martin, today is Friday 13.11.2026. Today's birthdays are 🎂: • Anna Smith (36)
Read-only access Nothing stored Private to Martin

Who can use it

Only Martin with his own Google account. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

What it never does

  • Create, change or delete a contact.
  • Share contact data with anyone else.
  • Use contact data for advertising or AI training.
